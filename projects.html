GRPO from Scratch

RL fine-tuning of an LLM

What does a language model learn when the grader that pays its reward is more lenient than the real objective? I implemented GRPO from scratch and fine-tuned Qwen2.5-0.5B on GSM8K four times, changing only the reward grader. Trained on the strict grader, test accuracy rose from 9.1% to 52.3%. Trained on lenient graders, the model got better at the arithmetic but stopped writing the required answer tag, so its strict accuracy fell to about 0%. A small format bonus was the worst: within ten steps the model stopped reasoning and wrote only a tag with a guessed number.

Multimodal Semantic IDs

Generative recommendation

A generative recommender gives every product a short code and generates the code of the next product a user will buy. I built one in the style of TIGER on Amazon Reviews 2023 and compared four ways of building the codes from product text and images. Late fusion beat text-only codes in all four settings, for example NDCG@10 from 0.0294 to 0.0332, while early fusion did not help. Two results went against me: no generative model retrieved a single cold item, and a plain SASRec fed the same embeddings beat every generative model.

Brain Tumor Detection in MRI

Detector comparison

I compared a one-stage detector, a two-stage detector and a transformer on 5,249 MRI slices with four tumor classes, all under one data pipeline and one COCO evaluation. YOLOv8m reached 0.781 mAP@0.5:0.95, against 0.562 for Faster R-CNN and 0.508 for DETR. Faster R-CNN is close to YOLOv8 at IoU 0.5 and far behind at IoU 0.75: it finds the tumors and draws loose boxes.
